# Canadian Coin Collection

Personal checklist of Canadian special and commemorative circulation coins. Open `index.html` with the bundled files or use GitHub Pages; no build step is needed.

## Collection and saved progress

The checklist contains **174 entries**, with **81 owned defaults**. Seven silver-composition issues were removed previously. The six nickel dollars explicitly removed by the owner are 1970 Manitoba, 1971 British Columbia, 1973 Prince Edward Island, 1974 Winnipeg, 1982 Constitution and 1984 Jacques Cartier. The owner also removed the 1943–1945 Victory Nickels and 1951 Identification of Nickel entries.

Existing coin IDs and the `alex-coins-v1` localStorage key remain unchanged. Saved true and false selections override defaults. Remaining entries are never renumbered, and new IDs 182–193 start missing. The erroneous 2005 Lucky Loonie (ID 110) is merged into the existing 2006 coin (ID 112); an owned selection on either entry is retained. [The Mint's 2006 annual report](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2010/mint/F71-2006-eng.pdf) explains that 2006-dated Lucky Loonies were also produced in 2005. The existing 2006 coin has its own photograph.

Search, denomination filters, Missing only, Coloured only, progress and photo enlargement remain available. Versioned script and image URLs refresh changed assets without clearing saved progress.

## Variant audit — 7 October 2026

The audit covers intentional commemorative circulation designs, colour/non-colour counterparts and the Arctic reverse designs (both frosting finishes are grouped per owner preference). It excludes collector-only colourizations, precious-metal collector issues, accidental paint loss, die errors and ordinary annual or mintmark varieties. The owner confirmed circulation-only scope; the collector-only coloured Canada Day 2002 quarter is excluded.

Twelve entries were added:

- Supreme Court of Canada (2025): colour.
- Alexander Graham Bell (2022): colour.
- Olympic Moments (2009): colour Cindy Klassen, men's hockey and women's hockey. The existing IDs retain their non-colour selections.
- Legendary Nature (2011): colour peregrine falcon, wood bison and orca. The orca's coin date is corrected from its 2012 release year to 2011.
- Canada 150 — Hope for a Green Future (2017): non-colour.
- Arctic Expedition and Life in the North (2013): their second frosting finishes were identified, then grouped into one card per design at the owner’s request on 8 October.
- In Flanders Fields (2015): the previously missing $2 circulation coin.

The 2022 black-ring memorial toonie was already ID 165. It now has a direct Mint image and the searchable name **Black Toonie – Queen Elizabeth II Memorial**, without duplicating the coin or losing its saved selection.

Both colour/non-colour versions are listed for the modern Bluenose dime; the Olympic Moments, Legendary Nature, War of 1812, 2015 Flag/Poppy and 2017 Canada 150 quarters; Bell, Klondike, Peterson, MacGill, Montgomery, Supreme Court and FIFA dollars; and the colour-issued commemorative toonies from Canada 150 through the 2026 Legion issue. Earlier Poppy and Breast Cancer circulation quarters have no intentional unpainted counterpart in this scope.

Primary references: [Legendary Nature 12-pack](https://www.mint.ca/en/shop/coins/2011/canadian-legendary-nature-coin-25-cent-12-pack-2011), [Olympic Moments — Mint's 2009 report](https://www.mint.ca/globalassets/about/company/reports/2009/2009-annual-report_world-class-performance.pdf), [quarter design/variant infographic](https://www.mint.ca/globalassets/learn/blog/2022/l5-discover-canadas-25-cent-coin/2022-087-corp_social---25-cent-infographic_eng.pdf), [toonie design/variant infographic](https://www.mint.ca/globalassets/learn/blog/2023/l5-toonie/toonie-infographic), [Bell](https://www.mint.ca/en/company/media-room/2022-10-20-the-royal-canadian-mint-celebrates-scientific-innovation-with-new-1-circulation-coin-commemorating-legendary-inventor-alexander-graham-bell), [Supreme Court colour roll](https://www.mint.ca/en/shop/coins/2025/2025-1-150th-anniversary-of-the-supreme-court-of-canada-coloured-special-wrap-roll), [black toonie](https://www.mint.ca/en/shop/coins/2022/2-special-wrap-roll-honouring-queen-elizabeth-ii), and [2015 In Flanders Fields authorization](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p2/2015/2015-07-01/html/sor-dors153-eng.html).

## Photographs

All 174 entries now have bundled photographs of their listed variant. Exact non-colour photographs were added for Tecumseh (82), End of WWII (158, CNumis vintage 210154), and Arctic Expedition frosted compass (191). Imaginaire calls the latter brilliant finish, referring to its reflective explorers; this contrasts with the existing frosted-explorers coin (83). Bell colour (183) now uses the Mint's front-facing image. The Coloured only toggle combines with search, denomination and Missing only; it excludes unpainted coins and the black-ring toonie.

Photos are credited to CNumis, Wikimedia Commons contributors, Royal Canadian Mint and Imaginaire. Original imagery and embedded notices are preserved. Mint and Imaginaire imagery is included at the owner's request for this personal noncommercial checklist. `image-sources.json` and `images.js` record matching local paths, source pages, original URLs and SHA-256 checksums. Source attribution does not imply ownership. Older unused bundled images are retained for recovery.

## Circulation and reference review — 8 October 2026

The owner removed the remaining 1943/1944 Victory Nickels. Arctic Expedition (83 → 191) and Life in the North (192 → 84) now each occupy one card; any saved true selection on either finish is retained. Intentional colour/non-colour counterparts remain separate. The checklist now has 174 collectible entries (3 nickels, 5 dimes, 88 quarters, 35 loonies, 43 toonies) and five separate standard-design references. References have no collection checkbox and do not affect counts or progress; each denomination filter starts with its reference, while Missing only and Coloured only hide references.

The five reference photographs are original front-facing Mint images of the beaver, Bluenose, caribou, common loon and polar bear, from the [2026 standard circulation rolls](https://proddep.mint.ca/en-us/shopping/archives/2026/special-wrap-roll-collection-first-strikes-2026-pack-254059). The packaging is sold to collectors, but these are standard base-metal circulation designs, not silver collector coins. Their original image URLs and checksums are in the manifest.

Original two-sided images for Brock (80), Tecumseh (82), Arctic Expedition (191), and In Flanders Fields (193) are displayed through a clipped reverse-only viewport, including enlargement. Source image files remain unchanged.

The list was compared with the Mint's [current commemorative circulation program](https://www.mint.ca/en/discover/canadian-commemorative-circulation-program), [quarter infographic](https://www.mint.ca/globalassets/learn/blog/2022/l5-discover-canadas-25-cent-coin/2022-087-corp_social---25-cent-infographic_eng.pdf), [toonie infographic](https://www.mint.ca/globalassets/learn/blog/2023/l5-toonie/toonie-infographic), and denomination histories: [5¢](https://www.mint.ca/en/discover/canadian-circulation/5-cents), [10¢](https://www.mint.ca/en/discover/canadian-circulation/10-cents), [25¢](https://www.mint.ca/en/discover/canadian-circulation/25-cents), [$1](https://www.mint.ca/en/discover/canadian-circulation/1-dollar), [$2](https://www.mint.ca/en/discover/canadian-circulation/2-dollars). No further missing commemorative reverse design was identified in these sources within the owner's scope. This is a design checklist, not a catalogue of annual dates, obverses, mintmarks, errors or every finish. The owner's excluded historical nickels/dollars remain excluded, along with silver-composition and collector-only issues, discontinued pennies, and 50-cent coins not normally found in change. Older circulation issues in the current denominations may be scarce in change; availability is not guaranteed.

## Coin stories and random discovery

Every checklist coin and standard reference has a shareable page at `coin.html?id=<stable-id>`. `stories.js` contains English issue, occasion, reverse-design and variant notes, with source links. Years are used unless a specific release date is documented; known differences between a coin date and its circulation release are explained in the issue note. These are concise original summaries rather than copied source descriptions.

The header's Random coin button samples all 174 checklist entries, excluding references. On a story page it excludes the current coin. `collection.js` shares the existing `alex-coins-v1` storage format and ownership migrations between both pages; standard references never contribute to progress. Photos retain their existing credits and reverse-only crops.
